Requirements Before Fubo TV Login
==================================

Before starting the Fubo TV login process, make sure you have:

* A compatible Smart TV.
* A stable internet connection.
* The Fubo TV application installed on your TV.
* An active Fubo TV account.
* Your registered email address.
* Your Fubo TV account password.
* A smartphone, tablet, or computer if an activation code is required.

How to Login to Fubo TV on Smart TV
===================================

Follow these steps to sign in to your Fubo TV account:

* Turn on your Smart TV.
* Connect your Smart TV to a stable internet connection.
* Open the application store on your television.
* Search for the Fubo TV application.
* Download and install the Fubo TV app.
* Open the application after installation.
* Select **Sign In**.
* Enter your registered Fubo TV email address.
* Enter your account password.
* Follow any additional instructions displayed on the screen.
* Complete the sign-in process.
* Once authenticated, your Fubo TV account should open on your Smart TV.

How to Activate Fubo TV on Smart TV
===================================

Some Smart TV models may display an activation code when you select the sign-in option. If an activation code appears, follow these steps:

* Open the Fubo TV app on your Smart TV.
* Select **Sign In**.
* Look for the activation code displayed on your TV screen.
* On another device, open the Fubo TV login or activation page.
* Enter the activation code when requested.
* Sign in with your Fubo TV account credentials.
* Follow the on-screen activation instructions.
* Return to your Smart TV.
* Wait for the Fubo TV app to refresh.
* Your account should then be connected to the Smart TV.

How to Fix Fubo TV Login Problems on Smart TV
==============================================

Sometimes you may experience problems while trying to sign in to Fubo TV. Login issues can occur because of incorrect account information, internet connectivity problems, outdated applications, or temporary app errors.

Try the following troubleshooting steps:

* Check your Smart TV internet connection.
* Verify that your Fubo TV email address is correct.
* Re-enter your account password carefully.
* Restart the Fubo TV application.
* Restart your Smart TV.
* Check for available Fubo TV app updates.
* Update your Smart TV software.
* Restart your Wi-Fi router.
* Reinstall the Fubo TV application if necessary.
* Try signing in again.

Fubo TV App Not Working on Smart TV
===================================

If the Fubo TV application is not opening, freezing, or closing unexpectedly, first check your internet connection. A weak or unstable connection can affect the performance of the application.

Restart your Smart TV and open the Fubo TV app again. You should also check whether the application or Smart TV software requires an update.

If the problem continues, uninstalling and reinstalling the Fubo TV application may help resolve temporary application problems.

Forgot Fubo TV Password
=======================

If you cannot remember your Fubo TV account password, use the password recovery option available on the Fubo TV sign-in page.

Follow the password reset instructions, create a new secure password, and then return to your Smart TV. Enter the updated credentials to sign in to your Fubo TV account.
